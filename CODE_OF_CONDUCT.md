# Code of Conduct

We value respectful, inclusive, and collaborative behavior. All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to follow these guidelines.

Be Respectful
- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Assume good intent and be constructive in feedback.

Be Inclusive
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels.
- Avoid exclusionary language and behavior.

Prohibited Behavior
- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory comments will not be tolerated.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or persistent disruptive behavior are prohibited.

Reporting
- If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers by emailing `maintainers@yourdomain.com` or opening a confidential issue.
- Replace the contact address above with a maintainer or security contact for this project.

Consequences
- Maintainers may remove content or block users who violate this policy.
- Violations may be escalated to repository owners or hosting platform support.

Acknowledgements
- This document is a short, project-specific code of conduct. Consider adopting a formal policy such as the Contributor Covenant and linking to it here.
